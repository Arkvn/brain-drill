What this is

An offline, single-file re-implementation of several free online trainers published at vomikod.com. No code, images or texts from that site are used: the site documents only the names and settings of its trainers, so the mechanics here are my interpretation of those settings. Built-in texts are original and come in parallel Russian and English versions.

How to read the numbers

Reading speed is words in the text divided by your reading time. A large review puts typical silent reading of English non-fiction at about 238 words per minute (Brysbaert, 2019). Treat these drills as attention and decoding practice: research reviews find that pushing reading speed far above the normal rate costs comprehension, and that the bottleneck is language processing, not eye movement (Rayner et al., 2016).

Keyboard

  • Running line: Space pauses and resumes.
  • Magic square: type digits into the highlighted cells, Tab moves to the next cell, Enter checks the square.
